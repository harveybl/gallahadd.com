#### Important product note

Mobile Comfy Creator connects to servers and GPUs you own or choose — including your ComfyUI / SwarmUI host, optional Vast.ai remote GPUs, optional Tailscale/VPN, and optional Ollama. Gallahadd does not operate those servers. We generally cannot reset your remote workflows, recover files from your GPU host, or change third-party account settings. For issues on those systems, check your host logs and your provider’s support channels.

#### Diagnostics & privacy (short)

Crash and analytics events are stored on your device via LocalAnalyticsProvider. They are not sent to Gallahadd or to third-party analytics vendors unless you email them to support. There is no third-party analytics / MMP collection. See the Privacy Policy for details.
