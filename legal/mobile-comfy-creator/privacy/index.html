1. Our product model (Mobile Comfy Creator) — stated honestly

Mobile Comfy Creator is a client that helps you connect to infrastructure you own, run, or choose:

  • ComfyUI and/or SwarmUI servers that you operate or control — not operated by Gallahadd.
  • Optional remote GPU instances from Vast.ai (or similar providers you choose) that you select and configure — third-party; Gallahadd does not run those GPUs.
  • Optional Tailscale, VPN, or similar connectivity that you configure — third-party / user-controlled.
  • Optional Ollama (or similar) local or user-run LLM endpoints — user-controlled.
  • Optional Apple Intelligence on-device features when available on your device — processing is subject to Apple’s on-device / Apple-controlled features and Apple’s terms and privacy practices. Gallahadd does not invent or restate Apple’s internal practices beyond this high-level description.
  • Photo Library access (read and/or add) only as needed for workflows that use images you select — requested when a feature needs it. Where that image data is processed depends on where you send the work (your local server, a Vast.ai GPU you chose, on-device features, etc.).

What Gallahadd does not do under this model:

  • Gallahadd does not operate a company backend that hosts your ComfyUI/SwarmUI workflows as a Gallahadd-managed service.
  • Gallahadd does not run an ads backend for this App.
  • Gallahadd is not the processor of prompts, images, models, or workflow data once that content leaves your device to your chosen endpoints (ComfyUI, SwarmUI, Vast.ai or similar, Ollama, etc.). Those endpoints’ operators (often you, or a third party you contracted) control that processing.
  • The App does not use iCloud sync for MCC settings or content under the current product model.
  • The App does not request a Camera permission key.

What this means: Most generative-AI content you create or send through the App is handled by systems you connect to. Gallahadd’s access to that content is limited as described below (typically support messages you choose to send, including any local diagnostics you attach).

2. Information we may handle

#### 2.1 Information you provide directly to Gallahadd

If you contact us (email to support@gallahadd.com or similar), we may receive:

  • your email address and name (if provided);
  • the content of your message;
  • any attachments, screenshots, logs, or on-device diagnostic exports you choose to send (these may include workflow details, prompts, images, or local crash/event records — only if you attach them).

We use this information to respond to support and legal requests.

#### 2.2 Information processed on your device or sent to your chosen endpoints (not a Gallahadd content backend)

Depending on features you use, the App may:

  • store connection settings, preferences, and UI state on your device (not synced via iCloud under the current model);
  • send prompts, images, models, workflow graphs, or related data to endpoints you configure (ComfyUI, SwarmUI, Vast.ai or similar remote GPU providers you choose, Ollama, etc.);
  • use Photo Library permissions (read and/or add) when you select or save images for a workflow;
  • use Local Network, Microphone, Speech Recognition, and Notifications permissions as needed for App features you use;
  • optionally invoke Apple Intelligence or other Apple on-device APIs when available on your device and when you use those features — subject to Apple’s terms and privacy practices.

Gallahadd does not receive a copy of your generative prompts/images/workflows through a Gallahadd-operated content API under the product model in Section 1. Content that leaves the device to your servers or third-party GPUs (including Vast.ai) is governed by those operators’ practices and your agreements with them — not by a Gallahadd content-processing backend.

#### 2.3 App Store / Apple commerce

Purchases, subscriptions, and refunds (if any) are processed by Apple. Apple’s privacy practices apply to payment data. Gallahadd may receive limited non-payment reports from Apple (e.g., sales/trends in App Store Connect) that generally do not include your full payment card details.

#### 2.4 Website (gallahadd.com)

When you visit the Site, standard web server or hosting logs may collect IP address, browser type, and pages requested. We do not invent cookie banners, trackers, or processors beyond what is actually deployed on the Site.

#### 2.5 Diagnostics & analytics (MCC eng audit accepted)

On-device only. No third-party analytics or MMP.

Mobile Comfy Creator uses an on-device LocalAnalyticsProvider. Crash reports and analytics events are stored locally on your device. They do not leave your device unless you choose to email them to Gallahadd support (for example, by attaching a diagnostic export to a message to support@gallahadd.com).

  • No third-party analytics SDK collects data from the App.
  • No MMP / attribution SDK is used.
  • Code classes named for vendors such as Sentry or PostHog may exist in the project as unwired mocks for development scaffolding. They are not connected to collect or transmit data. Gallahadd does not claim that Sentry, PostHog, or any similar third-party service collects analytics or crash data from Mobile Comfy Creator.

Apple may also provide limited diagnostic information through Apple’s standard App Store / platform mechanisms (for example, crash reports you share with developers via Apple), subject to your device and Apple Account settings.

#### 2.6 Information we do not intentionally collect via a Gallahadd ads or content backend

Based on the stated product model, Gallahadd does not:

  • operate an ads backend or advertising network integration for this App;
  • operate a Gallahadd-hosted generative content store for your ComfyUI/SwarmUI jobs;
  • sell your generative content from a Gallahadd server we do not run;
  • sync MCC data via iCloud under the current model.

iOS permissions used (as audited): Local Network; Microphone; Speech Recognition; Photo Library (read and/or add); Notifications. No Camera permission key. Permissions are for App features you use — not for Gallahadd advertising.

4. How we use information

We use information described in Section 2.1 to:

  • provide support;

5. Children’s privacy

Mobile Comfy Creator is not directed to children under 13 (or higher age where required). The Terms of Use require users to be at least 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Gallahadd backend. If you believe a child provided information via support email, contact support@gallahadd.com to request deletion.

Generative-AI tools may produce content inappropriate for minors; see Terms of Use acceptable-use rules. Parental controls and device settings are the user’s / guardian’s responsibility.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device App settings / caches / LocalAnalyticsProvider data: You control deletion by clearing App data or deleting the App, subject to your device backups.
  • Data on your ComfyUI / SwarmUI / Vast.ai / Ollama endpoints: Controlled by you and those operators — Gallahadd cannot delete data on servers we do not operate.
  • Support emails (and any diagnostics you attach): Retained as needed to provide support and meet legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized.
  • Gallahadd has no company-side MCC generative-content account database to “delete” under the Section 1 model. If that changes, this Policy must be updated before launch of any such backend feature.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures for systems we control (e.g., support inbox access controls). Security of your servers, Tailscale/VPN tunnels, Vast.ai instances, and API keys is your responsibility. On-device security also depends on your device passcode and Apple Account. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Keys and endpoints: If the App stores connection URLs, tokens, or credentials on device, protect your device and do not share screenshots of secrets.

8. International users

Gallahadd Software LLC is based in Waxahachie, Texas, United States. If you use the App from other regions:

  • content you send to your servers or third-party GPUs (including Vast.ai) may be processed in whatever region those endpoints use;
  • Apple platform features follow Apple’s infrastructure and your settings;
  • support email retained by Gallahadd may be processed in the U.S.

9. Your privacy rights (U.S. state / other)

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete, or correct personal information Gallahadd holds (typically support correspondence and any diagnostics you emailed). To exercise: email support@gallahadd.com.

Because generative and workflow content under the stated model is processed on your infrastructure (or third parties you choose, including Vast.ai), many access/delete requests for that content must be fulfilled via your servers and provider dashboards — not by Gallahadd server export.
