Product model in one line
Mobile Comfy Creator is a client that connects you to ComfyUI, SwarmUI, optional Vast.ai GPUs, optional Tailscale/VPN, and optional Ollama that you own or choose. Gallahadd does not operate those servers, does not run an ads backend, and does not process your prompts/images/workflows on a Gallahadd content backend. 2. Our Product Model

  • ComfyUI and/or SwarmUI servers — operated by you, not Gallahadd.
  • Optional remote GPUs from Vast.ai (or similar) — third-party; you configure them; Gallahadd does not run those GPUs.
  • Optional Tailscale/VPN — third-party, user-controlled.
  • Optional Ollama — user-controlled.
  • Optional Apple Intelligence when available on your device — subject to Apple’s terms and privacy practices.
  • Photo Library (read and/or add) when a feature needs images you select.

Gallahadd does not: operate a ComfyUI/SwarmUI hosting backend; run an ads backend for this App; use iCloud sync for MCC under the current model; or request a Camera permission key.

Prompts, images, models, and workflows sent to endpoints you configure leave the device to your infrastructure (or third parties you chose). Those operators control that processing.
