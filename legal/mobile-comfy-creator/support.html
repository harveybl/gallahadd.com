Important Product Note

Mobile Comfy Creator connects to servers and GPUs you own or choose — including your ComfyUI / SwarmUI host, optional Vast.ai remote GPUs, optional Tailscale/VPN, and optional Ollama. Gallahadd does not operate those servers. We generally cannot reset your remote workflows, recover files from your GPU host, or change third-party account settings. For issues on those systems, check your host logs and your provider’s support channels.

Diagnostics & Privacy

Crash and analytics events are stored on your device via LocalAnalyticsProvider. They are not sent to Gallahadd or to third-party analytics vendors unless you email them to support. There is no third-party analytics / MMP collection. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Gallahadd fix my ComfyUI / SwarmUI / Vast.ai server?

Usually no. Those systems are yours or your provider’s. We can help with App-side connection settings and troubleshooting steps, but we cannot log into or administer your remote host.

Does the App send analytics to Gallahadd automatically?

No. LocalAnalyticsProvider keeps crash/events on device until you choose to email them to support. Sentry/PostHog-named classes in the codebase (if present) are unwired mocks and do not collect data.

What permissions does the App use?

Local Network, Microphone, Speech Recognition, Photo Library (read/add), and Notifications — as needed for features you use. No Camera permission key. No iCloud sync under the current model. Apple Intelligence may be used when available on your device.

Purchases & refunds

If you purchased through the App Store, billing and refunds are handled by Apple. Manage subscriptions and request refunds via your Apple Account / reportaproblem.apple.com as applicable.
