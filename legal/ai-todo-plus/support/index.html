Frequently Asked Questions

How do AI workouts work?

Open the AI Workouts flow, enter your goals, available equipment, and time constraints. The App sends a prompt to the AI provider to generate a plan with exercises, sets, reps, and instructions. Generated content can sync across your devices if iCloud sync is enabled.

Can I use the App offline?

Yes for local tasks and previously generated content. AI generation requires an internet connection. Once created, items generally work offline on the device.

Does the App use Apple Health / HealthKit?

Only if you turn on Save to Apple Health in Nutrition settings. The App then writes the food you log (calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber, sugar, sodium and saturated fat) to Apple Health. It never reads any Health data. You can revoke access anytime in the Health app (Sharing › Apps). The Privacy Policy (section 2.4) has the details.

Why aren’t my tasks syncing?

Sign into the same Apple ID on each device, enable iCloud, and ensure AI Todo Plus is allowed under iCloud settings. Sync can take a few minutes. Check free iCloud storage.

How do recurring tasks work?

When creating or editing a task, use the Repeat option (daily, weekly, monthly, or custom) if available in your version.

Can I export my data?

If your version includes export/backup in Settings, use that. Otherwise email support@gallahadd.com and we will point you to the available options.

Is my data private?

Tasks are stored on your device and optionally in your iCloud. AI prompts for generation features are sent to the AI provider for processing. The App may collect usage analytics and crash diagnostics for product improvement (see the Privacy Policy and App Store privacy labels). We do not sell your personal information. See the full Privacy Policy.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Use your Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deleting the App does not cancel billing. Refunds are handled by Apple.
