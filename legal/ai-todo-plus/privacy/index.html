Your Privacy Matters
AI Todo Plus is operated by Gallahadd Software LLC. 2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Task Data: Tasks, notes, due dates, priorities, and categories you create
  • AI Prompts: The details you enter for an AI request you start (for example a task to break down into steps, text to split into separate tasks, meal, workout and grocery preferences, and AI chat messages), and food descriptions and meal photos you add in Food › Describe or Photo
  • Nutrition Data: Foods you log (name, brand, amount, meal, date and time, and nutrition values), your saved foods, meals and recipes, and your optional daily nutrition goals
  • Food Search Terms and Barcodes: Text you type into food search and barcodes you scan or enter
  • Account / Apple context: Apple ID context used for iCloud sync (handled by Apple), and purchase context via StoreKit: your in-app purchases (Food & Menu, Fitness, or both), any past Basic or Premium subscription, and Apple’s signed record of when you first downloaded the App (see 4.6). Apple processes payment.
  • Optional API Keys: Your personal OpenAI API key and/or USDA FoodData Central (api.data.gov) key if you choose to use your own (stored in the device Keychain, never sent to Gallahadd)

2.2 Information That May Be Collected Automatically

The App may collect the following automatically to operate, secure, and improve the product:

  • No usage analytics: The App does not send usage statistics or AI feedback to Gallahadd or anyone else. Ratings you give AI results stay on your device.
  • Device Information: iOS version, device type, and app version (as needed for support and compatibility)
  • Crash Diagnostics: Apple’s crash and performance reports, only if you choose to share analytics with app developers (iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements). The App has no crash reporting service of its own.
  • Purchase Data: Your purchase and subscription history for the App, read on your device from Apple StoreKit to unlock what you own (see 4.6). Gallahadd does not receive payment details.

2.3 Information We Do Not Seek to Collect by Default

  • Location data (unless a future feature requires it and you grant permission)
  • Contact lists
  • Photos or media, except a meal photo you choose or take in Food › Describe or Photo, which is sent only when you tap the Estimate button that names where it goes: directly to OpenAI with your own API key (see 4.2), or, if AI Todo Plus AI is your chosen AI, through that service (see 4.3). Apple Intelligence can’t look at photos, so photo estimates are never made with Apple Intelligence. Barcode and nutrition-label scanning and label text recognition run on your device, and those images are not stored or uploaded.
  • Browsing history outside the App
  • Health / HealthKit data from Apple Health. The App never reads data from Apple Health (see 2.4).

2.4 Apple Health (Optional, Write-Only)

If you turn on Save to Apple Health in Settings › Food and grant permission, the App writes the food you log to Apple Health as dietary energy, protein, carbohydrates, total fat, fiber, sugar, sodium and saturated fat (with the food name, time, and identifiers that let the App update or remove its own entries). The App:

  • Requests write (share) permission only and never reads any Health data, including steps, heart rate, weight or data written by other apps
  • Does not send Health data to Gallahadd, OpenAI, or any other third party, and does not use it for advertising or marketing
  • Updates or deletes the Health entry it wrote when you edit or delete the logged food on the device that logged it

You can change or revoke this permission at any time in the Health app (Sharing › Apps) or iOS Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices, and turn off Save to Apple Health in the App. Entries already saved stay in Apple Health until you delete them there.

2.5 Calendar Sync (Optional)

Calendar Sync is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings › Calendar Sync and grant calendar access, the App writes the title, notes and due date of each open task that has a due date to a calendar named “AI Todo Plus” through iOS Calendar, so it can appear on your other devices and on wall displays that subscribe to your iCloud Calendar. The App only reads and changes events in that calendar; it does not read or change your other calendars and does not send calendar data to Gallahadd, OpenAI or any other third party. You can turn it off in the App, revoke access in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars, and delete the “AI Todo Plus” calendar in the Calendar app.

4. AI and Third-Party Services

Your choice of AI. You choose the AI in Settings › AI: Apple Intelligence (4.1), your own OpenAI API key (4.2), or, only for customers who had it before app version 2.0, AI Todo Plus AI (4.3). Nothing is sent for AI unless you start a request (for example breaking down a task, splitting text into tasks, meal, workout or grocery planning, AI chat, or Food › Describe and Photo), and then only the details you enter for that request. A request is never sent to an AI you did not choose: if a request fails, the App may offer the other AI options you have set up as buttons (for example “Try with your OpenAI key”), and sends nothing until you tap one.

4.1 Apple Intelligence

When Settings › AI is set to Apple Intelligence (the default on iPhones that support it), AI requests you start are answered by Apple’s on-device model when they fit, and do not leave your device. Larger requests are sent to Apple’s Private Cloud Compute (iOS 27 or later). Apple states that it designed Private Cloud Compute so that requests are used only to answer them and are not stored or made accessible to anyone, including Apple; see Apple’s Private Cloud Compute documentation and privacy policy. Apple’s processing follows Apple’s terms and policies, and Apple may apply its own usage limits. Gallahadd and OpenAI do not receive these requests or their results. Results are labeled “Made on device” or “Made with Apple Private Cloud Compute”. When Food › Describe runs on device and you are online, it may look up short food names (up to six words each) in Open Food Facts or USDA FoodData Central, the same lookups as a food search (see 4.4).

4.2 Your Own OpenAI Key

If you choose your own OpenAI API key, the key is stored in your device’s Keychain and never sent to Gallahadd. The details you enter for each AI request (for Food › Describe, your food description or meal photo and the meal it is for) go directly from your device to OpenAI with your key and never pass through Gallahadd. Each request asks OpenAI not to store it (store: false). A meal photo is reduced in size and sent without its location or other photo details, only when you tap the Estimate button that names your key. OpenAI processes these requests as an independent provider under your agreement with OpenAI; your OpenAI account’s data controls and OpenAI’s privacy policy apply, and OpenAI may keep data as its policies allow. OpenAI bills you for this use.

4.3 AI Todo Plus AI (Existing Customers Only)

AI Todo Plus AI is a hosted AI service we offered before app version 2.0. It is not available to new customers. It remains available, for as long as they stay subscribed, to customers with an active Basic or Premium subscription, and through January 31, 2027 to customers who used the App before version 2.0. It is used only when you choose it in Settings › AI.

Each request you start goes to an AI service operated by Gallahadd on Cloudflare, Inc.’s network (Cloudflare Workers, in the United States and other Cloudflare locations), which forwards it to OpenAI and returns the answer. Cloudflare acts as our processor. This service does not store or log the content of your requests or of the answers. It keeps only what it needs to prevent abuse: an anonymous per-install identifier created by Apple’s App Attest (it does not identify you); that install’s request and token counts by day and by month (daily counts are deleted after about two days and monthly counts about 40 days after their month ends, at the install’s next request; an install that stops using the service keeps its last counts until the service is retired); and technical logs of the model, time, status and size of each request, kept for up to 7 days. To help OpenAI detect misuse, requests carry a one-way hash of that identifier. The service asks OpenAI not to store responses; OpenAI’s own retention and data-sharing terms still apply. Requests made through AI Todo Plus AI may be covered by OpenAI’s data-sharing program for its complimentary usage tiers, under which OpenAI may use those inputs and outputs to improve its models. A Food › Describe meal photo goes through this service only if AI Todo Plus AI is your chosen AI and you tap the Estimate button that names it; it is reduced in size, sent without its location or other photo details, and not stored by Gallahadd or on the service.

  • We do not store your prompts or the AI’s answers (per current product design)
  • Personal information in prompts should be minimized; avoid including unnecessary identifiers
AI Data Handling: We do not intentionally send your complete task list or personal identifiers to AI services. Only the specific prompt needed for the requested feature is transmitted.

4.4 Food Databases (USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts)

When you search for a food or scan a barcode while online, the search text or barcode is sent to the USDA FoodData Central API (U.S. Department of Agriculture, via api.data.gov) and/or Open Food Facts to return nutrition facts. These requests include the App name and version but no account, device or personal identifiers; like any internet request, the services receive your IP address. Without your own USDA key, USDA lookups use a shared demonstration key and run only when you tap Search or scan a barcode. The built-in food database works offline without any request.

4.5 iCloud Sync

When you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is on for the App, the App syncs through your private iCloud database:

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple under Apple’s iCloud practices
  • Syncs across devices signed into the same Apple ID (your tasks and your food log are private to your Apple ID; the App has no sharing with other people)
  • You can turn off iCloud for the App in iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud

4.6 Purchases (StoreKit)

  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple; payment details are never accessible to Gallahadd
  • To unlock what you own, the App reads on your device, from Apple StoreKit, your purchases of the Food & Menu and Fitness packs and any Basic or Premium subscription
  • To keep the packs free for customers who used the App before packs existed, the App also reads Apple’s signed record of the app version you originally downloaded, and checks once, on the first launch of version 2.0, whether Food or Fitness data was already on the device. If you installed before version 2.0, ever had Basic or Premium, or had that data, both packs are unlocked at no charge
  • These checks happen on your device; the results are kept in the App’s settings on that device and are not sent to Gallahadd
