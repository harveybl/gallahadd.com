Your Privacy Matters
AI Todo Plus is operated by Gallahadd Software LLC. The app is designed with privacy in mind: your tasks and related content stay under your control on your device and, if you enable it, in your iCloud account. We do not sell your personal information. This policy describes practices as we understand them for the current product; where something is not yet confirmed, we say so rather than invent it.

1. Who We Are

Gallahadd Software LLC (“Gallahadd,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates AI Todo Plus (the “App”). For privacy and data-protection purposes, Gallahadd Software LLC is the operator and data controller of personal information processed in connection with the App, except where Apple, OpenAI, or another third party is the independent controller of data you provide directly to them (for example, Apple ID / iCloud / StoreKit, or OpenAI if you use your own API key under their terms).

Contact for privacy requests: support@gallahadd.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Task Data: Tasks, notes, due dates, priorities, and categories you create
  • AI Prompts: Meal preferences, workout goals, and natural-language inputs you submit to AI features
  • Account / Apple context: Apple ID context used for iCloud sync (handled by Apple) and subscription status via StoreKit
  • Optional API Key: Your personal OpenAI API key if you choose to use your own (stored on device with platform protections)

2.2 Information That May Be Collected Automatically

The prior published policy described the following automatic collection. Treat these as product claims to keep accurate; confirm vendors and whether each is currently enabled before relying on them:

  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous or aggregated app usage statistics (features used, session duration) — confirm vendor and whether enabled
  • Device Information: iOS version, device type, app version (as needed for support and compatibility)
  • Crash Reports: Technical information when the app encounters errors — confirm vendor (e.g., Apple / third party) and whether enabled
  • Subscription Data: Purchase history and subscription status via Apple StoreKit (Apple processes payment)

2.3 Information We Do Not Seek to Collect by Default

  • Location data (unless a future feature requires it and you grant permission)
  • Contact lists
  • Photos or media (unless you explicitly add them to tasks)
  • Browsing history outside the App

Health data note: The prior Support page mentioned optional Apple Health integration. The prior Privacy Policy stated health data was not collected. Current App Store builds do not declare HealthKit usage strings. If a future version requests Health permission and you grant it, that data stays under Apple Health rules; otherwise health data is not collected.

4. AI and Third-Party Services

4.1 OpenAI Integration

When you use AI features (meal planning, workout generation, natural-language processing):

  • Your prompts are sent to OpenAI’s API for processing (or to OpenAI using your own API key, if you supply one)
  • OpenAI processes requests according to their privacy policy and applicable terms
  • We do not store your prompts after receiving the AI response (per current product design described in the prior policy)
  • Personal information in prompts should be minimized; avoid including unnecessary identifiers
  • You may use your own OpenAI API key for greater control over billing and access under OpenAI’s terms
AI Data Handling: We do not intentionally send your complete task list or personal identifiers to AI services. Only the specific prompt needed for the requested feature is transmitted.

4.2 iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync:

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple under Apple’s iCloud practices
  • Syncs across devices signed into the same Apple ID
  • Family shared folders sync to invited members only
  • You can disable sync in Settings

4.3 StoreKit (Subscriptions)

  • Subscription purchases are processed by Apple
  • We receive transaction / entitlement information needed to unlock features
  • Payment card details are never accessible to Gallahadd

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 We Do Not:

  • Sell your personal information
  • Share your data with advertisers for third-party advertising (no ads claimed in current product marketing)
  • Use your task content for marketing without your consent
  • Access your task content unless you explicitly share it for support

5.2 We May Share or Disclose Data:

  • With your consent: When you request or approve it
  • Family Sharing: Shared folders with invited members

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the App’s design:

  • Encryption in transit: Network communications use TLS where applicable
  • Local protections: Platform storage / Keychain for sensitive items such as optional API keys
  • Access control: Device and Apple ID authentication as provided by Apple
  • Updates: Security-related app updates as issues are identified

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
